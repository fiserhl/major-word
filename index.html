Your Privacy
Major Word does not track you. We do not collect any personal information, analytics, or usage data. The only data stored is on your own device (via localStorage) to save your game progress and personal statistics. No cookies are sent to any server. Because all data lives on your device, statistics will not carry over between different devices or browsers. If you clear your browser data, your stats will reset.
